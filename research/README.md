# Research

Working material behind the model. Not part of the spec.

- `evidence-log.md`: every citation and its verification status.
- `open-questions.md`: decisions taken, decisions still open, and known weaknesses.
- `drafts/`: superseded working documents, kept for provenance.

Nothing here is authoritative. If a claim in `framework/` depends on something in this directory, the claim should carry its own citation.
