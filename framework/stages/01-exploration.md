# Exploration

*Stage 1 of five*

**Identifying opportunities, understanding user needs and concerns, and setting initial guardrails.**

Nothing has been committed to yet. The organisation is looking at its own work and asking where AI would fit, which is a very different activity from having AI already and hunting around for somewhere to put it.

## What is happening

At this stage the unit of work is the question, not the tool. The team maps workflows, surfaces pains and concerns, and writes down the first guardrails. What makes this a stage and not a preamble is that you can do it well or badly. Doing it badly stays invisible until much later, when a pilot succeeds at something nobody needed.

You are establishing two things at once, and they are easy to confuse. The first is what AI could plausibly do in this context. The second is what this team actually needs done. Exploration finishes when you have established both separately, so the overlap between them becomes a finding instead of an assumption you started with.

## What it is not

- **Not the absence of AI.** A team where everyone uses assistants daily and nothing has been agreed is still in Exploration. Individual use is a fact about individuals. It says very little about where the organisation sits.
- **Not a procurement stage.** Choosing a tool before establishing the need is the failure this stage exists to prevent. It tends to produce artifacts that read exactly like a well-run Exploration, except for the dates.
- **Not a gate to pass once.** A team sits in Exploration per dimension, so Ethics can be well past it while Solution Fit has not started.

## How the lenses read it

Tuckman's forming and Satir's late status quo agree here, which they do not do at every stage. Forming describes a group that has come together and not yet tested itself: roles stated, politeness high, disagreement still below the surface. Late status quo describes routines that hold and still feel stable. Both say the same thing about Exploration. What looks like alignment has not been tested, so do not read it as agreement.

For Solution Fit, the acceptance lenses put performance expectancy first: do people believe AI would improve their own work? That belief belongs to the person doing the job, and it can be wrong in either direction.

## The characteristic stall

A team stalls in Exploration by producing every artifact of the stage without doing the enquiry. A backlog of use cases matched to known AI capabilities, a values statement nobody would disagree with, a well-attended kickoff with no concern recorded anywhere: all three pass a superficial reading. Each dimension's Exploration to Experimentation gate examines one of them, and each fails for the same reason. Nothing in the record could have come out differently.

## Leaving the stage

You leave Exploration one dimension at a time, by passing that dimension's Exploration to Experimentation gate. The four gates live in the dimension files under `framework/dimensions/`. A team never leaves the stage as a whole, and the scatter that produces across the five dimensions is the result you are looking for.
