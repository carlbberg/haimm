# Stages

Five stages, unchanged from v0.1. Presented sequentially, but iterative in practice: teams revisit earlier stages as tools, people and needs change.

| # | Stage | id | What characterises it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Exploration | `exploration` | Identifying opportunities, understanding user needs and concerns, and setting initial guardrails. |
| 2 | Experimentation | `experimentation` | Piloting AI tools at small scale to validate relevance and effectiveness. |
| 3 | Integration | `integration` | Embedding AI into workflows and establishing working norms between teams and AI tools. |
| 4 | Optimization | `optimization` | Scaling what worked, focused on measurable outcomes: productivity, quality, learning. |
| 5 | Continuous Evolution | `continuous-evolution` | Ongoing adaptation as organisational needs and technologies change. |

The ids are the stable machine-readable form of the stage names, for anything built on top of the model.

All five descriptions are v0.1's own, compressed. An earlier v0.2 note claimed Exploration had no description in the published article. It does; the gap was in a web capture, not in v0.1. See `archive/v0.1/SOURCE.md`.

## Note on Stage 5

In the v0.1 assessment example, no team is plotted in the Continuous Evolution column on any dimension. Stage 5 is aspirational rather than observed, which matters for the Optimization to Continuous Evolution gate in every dimension: those criteria are reasoned, not checked against a real team. Each of the five gate files says so in its own `rationale` field, and `playbook/running-an-assessment.md` repeats it under known weaknesses. It stays true in v0.2 and is the single largest untested part of the model.
