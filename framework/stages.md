# Stages

Five stages, unchanged from v0.1. Presented sequentially, but iterative in practice: teams revisit earlier stages as tools, people and needs change.

| # | Stage | id | What characterises it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Exploration | `exploration` | Assessing readiness, uncovering opportunities and risks, building a foundational understanding of where AI fits existing workflows. |
| 2 | Experimentation | `experimentation` | Piloting AI tools at small scale to validate relevance and effectiveness. |
| 3 | Integration | `integration` | Embedding AI into workflows and establishing working norms between teams and AI tools. |
| 4 | Optimization | `optimization` | Scaling what worked, focused on measurable outcomes: productivity, quality, learning. |
| 5 | Continuous Evolution | `continuous-evolution` | Ongoing adaptation as organisational needs and technologies change. |

The stage ids are the values used in `framework/gates/*.yaml`.

## Note on Stage 1

The v0.1 article introduces Stage 1 but its stages section jumps from the introduction to Stage 2, so Exploration has no standalone description in the published text. The row above fills that gap and should be reviewed against intent when the v0.1 text is decomposed into this repo.

## Note on Stage 5

In the v0.1 assessment example, no team is plotted in the Continuous Evolution column on any dimension. Stage 5 appears to be aspirational rather than observed, which matters when writing gate criteria for the Optimization to Continuous Evolution transition: those criteria have not been checked against a real team.
