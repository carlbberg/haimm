# Combined matrix

Stages across, dimensions down. This is the table the published figure renders.

All five rows are now populated. The four v0.1 rows are moved verbatim from the v0.1 figure (`archive/v0.1/figures/haimm-v0.1-stages-dimensions.png`) and have not been revised for v0.2 register or content; see each dimension's own file for status.

Cells describe a state rather than an activity, and run roughly eleven to sixteen words. Match that register when adding rows.

| Dimension | Exploration | Experimentation | Integration | Optimization | Continuous Evolution |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Solution Fit | Initial awareness of AI potential and alignment with user needs and workflows. | Small-scale co-creation and validation of AI solutions through limited use cases. | Refinement and embedding of AI solutions into workflows based on feedback. | Scaled AI systems delivering measurable, organization-wide impact. | Dynamic alignment of AI with changing goals, needs, and capabilities through regular reassessment. |
| **Knowledge & Context** | Context is tacit and session-bound; initial mapping of what the work depends on knowing. | First durable context artifacts appear, created and held by individuals. | Shared context becomes team-owned, with agreed terminology, named owners, and a review cadence. | Context referenced from reachable sources of record rather than copied, refreshed as they change. | Context managed as an asset, with ownership, refresh, and deprecation reviewed regularly. |
| Human-AI Collaboration | AI introduced as a tool with clear but evolving limitations; basic understanding established. | AI begins assisting with decision-making, offering suggestions beyond simple tasks. | AI becomes a somewhat trusted "colleague," with humans reviewing and approving its outputs. | AI achieves semi-autonomous operation in some areas with human oversight. | Human-AI relationships adapt as technology evolves, fostering innovative collaboration. |
| People | Employees engaged in identifying potential applications and sharing hopes and concerns. | Pilot projects co-created with stakeholders to address resistance and build confidence. | Teams supported with training and resources as workflows and roles adapt to AI integration. | Broad organizational acceptance and active participation in AI-driven workflows and celebration of first success cases. | Continuous upskilling and culture-building around human-AI collaboration. |
| Ethics | Initial ethical considerations and potential risks outlined via draft guardrails. | Ethical principles and guardrails tested and refined during pilot implementations. | Transparent governance mechanisms integrated into AI operations. | Proactive monitoring and mitigation of ethical issues in real-time. | Evolving ethical frameworks to address new challenges and emerging use cases. |
