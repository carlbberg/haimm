# Combined matrix

Stages run across and dimensions run down, and this is the table that the published figure renders.

It is also the only home of the cells. Dimension files describe their own stage progression in prose and link back here instead of restating their row, so each cell has exactly one place to change.

Cells describe a state, not an activity, and they run to roughly eleven to sixteen words. If you add a row, match that register. Each cell should also be recognisable from the gate that lets a team into it, because a reader who moves between the two ought to find the same claim twice and not two different ones.

The cells are deliberately stable. Change one and you silently invalidate every placement anyone has already made against it. Treat the wording as fixed unless the model itself has changed, and record it in `CHANGELOG.md` when it does.

| Dimension | Exploration | Experimentation | Integration | Optimization | Continuous Evolution |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Solution Fit | AI's potential is recognised; user research maps needs before any solution is chosen. | Small pilots run with the people who do the work, and feedback can still change them. | Reshaped by what the pilot found, and embedded so that using it takes no initiative. | Running beyond the team that built it, on documented setup, with impact measured and not asserted. | Need re-checked on a cadence, with solutions narrowed and retired as well as added. |
| Workflow | Workflows inherited and undescribed; AI considered task by task rather than structurally. | What a workflow is for can be stated independently of how it currently happens. | One workflow reconsidered from its purpose against a real alternative, outcome recorded either way. | At least one workflow's structure no longer corresponds to its predecessor's. | Reconsideration on a cadence; workflows appear and go as capability changes. |
| Knowledge & Context | Context is tacit and session-bound; initial mapping of what the work depends on knowing. | First durable context artifacts appear, created and held by individuals. | Shared context becomes team-owned, with agreed terminology, named owners, and a review cadence. | Context referenced from reachable sources of record rather than copied, refreshed as they change. | Context managed as an asset, with ownership, refresh, and deprecation reviewed regularly. |
| Human-AI Collaboration | A tool with stated limits, and an agreement about what it should not be given. | AI proposes as well as executes, and people disagree openly about what it should be given. | AI works as a coworker on defined tasks, under a review step the team agreed. | Semi-autonomous in defined areas, grounded without re-briefing, under oversight somebody designed instead of assumed. | The division of labour reopens as capability moves, and automation falls as well as rises. |
| People | Current routines still hold, and people are saying across departments what they hope and fear. | Pilots make the change concrete, pushback arrives, and the people affected help shape what ships. | Roles and routines shift and stability drops with them, with training and support provided through it. | Capability has spread past the early adopters; new joiners reach competence without the original builder's help. | Upskilling recurs instead of happening once, and a standing forum takes role and culture questions. |
| Ethics | Risks named for the team's own use cases, and the first guardrails drafted against them. | Guardrails meet real pilots and get revised, with ethics review happening before rollout and not after. | Governance sits inside how systems run, with named owners and documentation somebody keeps current. | Monitoring runs continuously and has caught something, handled as it surfaced and not at audit. | Guardrails change because something new arrived, and the team can see a risk before it lands. |
