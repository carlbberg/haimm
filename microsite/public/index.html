    03 / Go deeper

    Keep the human
    in the picture.

    HAIMM helps team leaders, practitioners, and facilitators have a more useful conversation about AI adoption. It brings the work, the people, and their shared understanding into view.

    Explore the open-source framework ↗
    What does HAIMM measure?

    HAIMM describes how a team works with AI across Solution Fit, Workflow, Knowledge & Context, Human-AI Collaboration, People, and Ethics. It complements technical and enterprise AI maturity models.

    How does this quick assessment work?

    Each answer maps directly to one of the five stage descriptions for that dimension. “I’m not sure” leaves it unplaced. We show those six independent selections without averaging them or capping one based on another.

    The questions and short labels are an educational adaptation of v0.4.0. They do not test the full gate criteria. The results suggest what to inspect next, and never claim that a criterion failed.

    How do we verify our profile?

    Inspect the team’s artifacts, observe real work, and speak with people in different roles. Work upward from Exploration, stopping at the first gate that fails. Passing a gate requires three of four criteria, including the core one.

    Read the assessment playbook ↗
    What should we do with the results?

    Choose one gate by its consequences for your team. Use the evidence criteria to turn the gap into a specific task, then reassess after a meaningful work cycle. There is no expectation that all six dimensions should reach the same stage.

    Read the action playbook ↗
    How established is the model?

    v0.4.0 is a complete specification with 24 gates and 96 criteria, but no field data yet. Its theory anchors are sourced; the model and its profile interpretations remain unvalidated. Workflow is the newest and most provisional dimension.
