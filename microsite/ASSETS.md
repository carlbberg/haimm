## Hero sculpture

Retained from the original design; no longer displayed or requested by the page.

File: `public/assets/haimm-sculpture.png` (1672 × 941).

Generated using the built-in Imagegen tool, then visually inspected. Prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept
> Asset type: premium editorial website hero artwork for HAIMM, an educational microsite about human-AI collaboration.
> Primary request: A fluid rich emerald green glass ribbon organically weaving through an architectural brushed silver and chrome lattice, evoking human adaptability meeting structured AI systems.
> Scene/backdrop: seamless near-black charcoal studio backdrop matching #101410.
> Style/medium: museum-quality sculptural 3D artwork, premium architectural product photography, tangible realistic materials, professional art direction suitable for an Awwwards website.
> Composition/framing: wide landscape 2048x1152 image. Compelling close-up with an asymmetrical sculpture concentrated on the right and center; generous uninterrupted dark negative space on the left for website copy. The glass ribbon has elegant organic twists and thick, polished edges; the metal lattice has a considered architectural geometry. Sculpture partly cropped at right edge for visual energy.
> Lighting/mood: dramatic controlled studio illumination, crisp silver reflections, translucent emerald refractions and restrained lime highlights, exquisite material detail.
> Color palette: near-black charcoal, rich emerald green, brushed silver and chrome, minimal lime.
> Constraints: exactly one finished image. No text, logos, letters, robots, brains, hands, UI, watermarks, or decorative symbols.
