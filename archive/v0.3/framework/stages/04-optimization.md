# Optimization

*Stage 4 of five*

**Scaling what worked, focused on measurable outcomes: productivity, quality, learning.**

The first stage whose claim is empirical. Everything before it can be evidenced by how the work was done. This one asserts that the work produced something.

## What is happening

What was embedded in one context runs beyond it. Oversight is designed rather than improvised, context is referenced from sources of record rather than copied between people, and the impact claim is measured instead of asserted.

The real test is transferability. The setup that made a pilot work is often a fit between AI and one team's accumulated context rather than between AI and the workflow, and the two are indistinguishable until someone else tries it. A second team reaching working value from documented setup alone is the cleanest evidence in the model, and it is the criterion most often skipped for logistical reasons.

## What it is not

- **Not a metrics threshold.** No metric is an entry condition for any stage, here or anywhere. Gates place a team; metrics say whether it is moving. The reasoning is Campbell's law: a measure that decides a placement stops measuring the thing it was chosen for. See `framework/metrics.md`.
- **Not headcount.** Wide usage across an organisation with the underlying capability held by one team is Integration reported at Optimization's scale.
- **Not stability.** Running well is the achievement of this stage and the trap of the next one.

## How the lenses read it

Tuckman's performing and Satir's integration both fall here, and they agree. Performing is a group working cohesively with minimal friction. Satir's integration is where new practice becomes routine and performance rises past the level it held before the change.

That agreement is worth treating carefully. Both lenses describe a state that feels settled, and both were built to describe groups moving toward a resting point. HAIMM's fifth stage denies there is one, which is where both lenses stop being useful.

## The characteristic stall

Measurement substituted for the thing measured. A dashboard of AI usage and satisfaction, rising steadily, with no record of an outcome that changed. Usage says people opened the tool; it does not say opening it was worth their time.

The second stall is governance that was accurate at launch. Documentation for a decision-making system describes what it did at go-live and has not been touched since, while the system changed underneath it. It passes an existence check and fails a currency one, which is what the Ethics gate at this transition asks about.

## Leaving the stage

Per dimension, by passing that dimension's Optimization to Continuous Evolution gate. The four gates are in the dimension files under `framework/dimensions/`. Those four are the least trustworthy criteria in the model: no assessment has placed a team past Optimization on any dimension, so they are reasoned rather than observed. See `05-continuous-evolution.md`.
