## Why they are committed

Committing them keeps the v0.1 archive complete and lets the v0.1 to v0.2 diff
be meaningful, which is the point of this directory.

## Rights decision

**Permission obtained for both third-party figures.** Recorded 3 September 2026
on the author's statement. The granting correspondence is held by the author and
is not filed in this repository; if the permission carries conditions on
attribution, medium, or commercial use, add them to this section, because this
file is where a later reader will look for them.

This resolves the blocker on making the repository public. It does not place the
figures under the repository's MIT licence, and cannot: the author is not the
rightsholder and has no standing to relicense either image. `LICENSE` covers the
text and the author's own figures. These two are used by permission and stay
outside it.

## Earlier decision, taken for v0.2

Option 1, partially, and only for the live model. Neither third-party figure is
carried into `framework/`. The v0.2 dimension files describe the content in
text and cite the source: the ten-level scale as Sheridan and Verplank (1978)
with the 2000 crossing attributed correctly, and Satir's five phases named
without the diagram, which is a third-party rendering rather than Satir's own.

That decision stands, and was taken when the rights position was unresolved.
The permission recorded above settles the separate question of the archived
copies; it does not change how the live model handles the content.
